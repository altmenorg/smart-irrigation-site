    Usage¶

    Once Smart Irrigation is set up, it works out every day how much water each zone is short of. What happens next is up to you: Smart Irrigation can open the valves itself, or hand the durations to an automation, a controller or another integration. These pages cover both, and what you see along the way.

    Reference¶

    What Smart Irrigation publishes in Home Assistant, for automations and for your own code.

    Page What it covers
    Entities The sensor of each zone and its attributes, and the hub's entities.
    Services The actions you can call: calculate, fetch the weather, set a water balance, postpone, run a program…
    Events What Smart Irrigation fires: a start, a skipped start, a program that started or finished, a leak.
    The output contract What is published, when it changes, and what is promised, for anyone driving the water from their own code.
