Smart Irrigation for Home Assistant

Water your garden according to its needs.

Smart Irrigation follows the water in each zone's soil, day by day: what the sun and wind took, what the rain gave back. It waters only the millimetres that are missing, and nothing when the sky has done the job.

What it does for you

Less water, healthier plants, nothing to babysit

Waters what is missing

Each zone has its own balance in mm. The run lasts exactly long enough to bring it back to full, from your sprinklers' flow.

Skips when the sky does it

Rain measured or forecast, frost, wind or a wet soil sensor: the run is held back, and the page says why.

Programs you can read

Steps, schedules at sunrise or after another program, cycle and soak. Every program draws itself before it runs.

Weather from where you are

Open-Meteo out of the box, no key. Or your own weather station, a rain gauge, a soil probe, mixed as you like.

Valves that close, always

It checks each valve opened and closed, uses the valve's own timer when it has one, and sets a failing zone aside.

Counts what really ran

Watered by hand or by a flow meter? It credits the water that went out, and warns you about a leak.

Set up with AI

Tell it about your garden. It does the configuration.

Turn on the assistant option and your Home Assistant voice or chat assistant can read and change Smart Irrigation: zones, programs, schedules, down to the options only power users touch. It checks a program before saving it and tells you what it is about to change.

How it works

A water balance per zone, the agronomists' way

The model is the FAO's reference method for crop water needs, used in agriculture worldwide. Smart Irrigation runs it every day, or hour by hour, for each zone.

balance = balance − ET₀ × Kc + rain + wateringBelow zero, the zone is short. 1 mm missing = 1 litre per m².

ET₀
What a reference lawn loses to sun, wind, heat and dry air today (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith).
Kc
How thirsty your plants are compared with that lawn, month by month. Pick "vegetables" or "hedge", it sets it.
rain
Measured or from the weather service. Showers too small to reach the roots are not counted.
soil
Sand drains fast, clay holds on. The soil you pick caps what the zone can keep.

Install

Three steps, a few minutes

Find it in HACS

Open it in HACS in one click, or search "Smart Irrigation" there and download it. No repository to add.

Add the integration

Restart Home Assistant, then Settings › Devices & services › Add integration.

Follow the assistant

Open Smart Irrigation in the sidebar. It asks for your zones and valves, and the weather is on from the start.

Coming from Irrigation Unlimited? Bring your controllers, zones and sequences over in one go, and keep your schedules.
